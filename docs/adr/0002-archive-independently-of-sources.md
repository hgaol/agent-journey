# Archive Journeys independently of source locations

AgentJourney owns an Independent Archive rather than mirroring coding agents' history directories. Once captured, a Journey and its Source Evidence remain available even if the original files are moved, rewritten, or deleted. All revisions are retained by default; removal requires an explicit AgentJourney action or user-authored Retention Policy and never occurs as silent cleanup. This consumes additional local storage and can retain content longer than its source, but prevents evidence from disappearing outside the platform's control.
