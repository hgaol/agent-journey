# Describe fidelity as capabilities rather than one tier

Each Interpretation carries a Fidelity Manifest describing content and timing coverage by Activity kind, Delivery Trace availability, Agent Thread and causal-link coverage, terminal-stream presence, and known gaps linked to its Coverage Report. The UI may derive concise Semantic, Timed, or Terminal Fidelity Badges, but those summaries do not imply uniform fidelity across the Journey. This is more detailed than a scalar tier, but avoids overstating evidence when native histories preserve different dimensions unevenly.
