Why AgentJourney

Agent work deserves
a durable record.

Coding agents already persist rich histories. AgentJourney preserves that evidence before source formats drift, sessions disappear, or important decisions get buried.

01

Evidence first

Exact Source Bundles stay immutable and byte-preserved. Canonical Activity can evolve without rewriting what the agent actually recorded.

Source Evidence ≠ interpretation
02

Replay, not reenactment

Recorded timing and delivery traces stay evidenced. Simulated typing and streaming are optional, explicit, and visibly labeled.

No invented forensic fidelity
03

Local custody

No accounts, cloud storage, or telemetry. The archive, browser rendering, search, HTML, and MP4 export stay on your machine.

Loopback only · no agent control

A passive workflow

From persisted history
to reviewable Journey.

  1. 01

    Approve a Source Root

    Grant read-only discovery to an agent history location. Manual scanning is the default; AgentJourney never launches or wraps the agent.

    ~/.pi/agent/sessions
  2. 02

    Preserve exact evidence

    Changed sessions are committed atomically into an independent, content-addressed local archive with provenance and coverage.

    Source Bundle · SHA-256
  3. 03

    Review the Activity Graph

    Search Journeys, inspect evidence, compare interpretations, follow tools and subagents, annotate findings, or replay evidenced timing.

    Review → Replay → Export
